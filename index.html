Postiz (self-hosted)

This is a private, self-hosted instance of the open-source Postiz social media scheduler. It is run by one person to schedule and upload videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Connects to YouTube channels owned by the operator through Google sign-in.
  • Uploads and schedules the operator's own videos, and reads basic channel information and analytics for those channels.
