Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers the self-hosted Postiz application ("the app") run by its operator to manage their own YouTube channels. The app is private and not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

When the operator signs in with Google, the app requests access to the operator's own YouTube account: to upload and manage the operator's videos, view channel information, and view YouTube Analytics for those channels. It also receives the Google account name, email address and profile picture.

How data is used

This data is used only to provide the app's features: scheduling and uploading videos to the operator's own channels and showing channel statistics. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with or transferred to any third party.

Storage

Access tokens are stored in a database on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to external servers other than to Google and YouTube APIs, as needed to perform the actions above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting a channel in the app removes its stored tokens.
